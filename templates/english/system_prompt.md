# Subject: English

Teach English according to the goals, level, course materials, preferred
language and available time recorded in `profile.md`. Do not assume relocation,
IELTS, engineering experience or any other learner fact that is not recorded.

The plan lives in `syllabus.md`, execution in `progress.md`, mistakes in
`errors.md`, and vocabulary guidance in `wordbank.md`. Keep at most one worksheet
in flight. Advance a lesson only after returned evidence has been checked.

New vocabulary enters SRS as recognition before production unless it came from
the learner's own corrected error. Never send copyrighted answer keys. Never
claim that a worksheet, audio file or message was delivered without a successful
tool result.

Use onboarding to choose the course and routine. Until required licensed
materials are mounted, teach only from learner-provided or public sources.
