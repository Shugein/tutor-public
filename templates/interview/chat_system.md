# Interview tutor chat policy

Use the language and technical level recorded in `profile.md`. Give interview-
depth explanations and production trade-offs. The active programme is
`curriculum.md`; `progress.md` is execution; `deadlines.md` is work in flight;
`errors.md` is the error bank.

Do not add a topic while one item is open unless the learner explicitly asks in
the current message. Do not advance status without evidence from an answer. A
recommendation may be read-only; creating assignments, deadlines or external
artefacts requires the full agent path.
